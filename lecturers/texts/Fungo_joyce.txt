Plenary Lecturer & Panelist
Joyce Estelle S. Fungo is an Assistant Professor of Philosophy at the University of the Philippines-Baguio and a PhD candidate at the School of Divinity, History, Philosophy, and Art History in the University of Aberdeen, supervised by Luca Moretti (University of Eastern Piedmont, Italy) and Federico Luzzi (University of Aberdeen, Scotland). Her course offerings in philosophy more recently have been in Special Questions in Ethics, Epistemology, and Metaphysics. Her academic interests rest in theories of knowledge and value and inquire into the enabling capacities of theory to better understand injustice and oppression in practice. Her hobbies shift at about the same rate as her moving from one place to another. But in various circles here and abroad, she’s often found herself in board game communities, some in casual dungeons and dragons circles, and at one point in a medieval history gathering. And I guess like everyone else these days, she occasionally plays pickleball. She believes in finding ways to approach ideological differences, including religious differences, through self-reflexivity and intellectual humility – two traits she herself struggles with but continually aspires to practice.
