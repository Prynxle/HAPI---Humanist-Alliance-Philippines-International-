Plenary Lecturer & Panelist
Chloe Nicole D. Piamonte teaches philosophy at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) and the Ateneo de Manila University (ADMU). She also serves as the Chief of PUP's Center for Social Inclusivity Studies where she leads funded research on gender, diversity, and inclusivity. Her commitment to the unimaginable weight of teaching at two different institutions, and doing research, is deeply motivated by ensuring that she is too exhausted to be knocked out by existential crisis. (Also, one pays rent and the other buys maintenance meds.) Most of Chloe's days are spent either on these pretentious academic initiatives or in the classroom convincing students that existential humanism is real philosophy. She likes to believe that the latter is crucial as a personal validation that specialising in the philosophical approaches of phenomenology and existentialism is still relevant. Her days off are spent playing games to justify her impulsive purchase of a handheld computer, visiting her psychiatrist and her surgeon (the "real" doctor), hospital confinement, or at home staring at the shameful pile of her moldy unread books. A friend once told her that her toxic trait is loving Heidegger.
