Plenary Lecturer
Darren E. Dumaop, PhD is an Associate Professor of Psychology and Associate Dean of the College of Liberal Arts at De La Salle University, where he serves as the inaugural Director of the Conflict in Religion Psychology Research Laboratory. He holds a PhD in Psychology, major in Social Psychology and minor in Personality, from the University of the Philippines Diliman. His research examines religious pluralism, religious conflict and its relationship with identity and agency, and prejudice against and mental health among Filipinos with secular identities. He is a former Chair of the Social Psychology Division of the Psychological Association of the Philippines and former Vice President of the Pambansang Samahan sa Sikolohiyang Pilipino. He is an alumnus of the UCSIA Summer School (Antwerp, Belgium) and a fellow of the Religion and Public Life in the Philippines Program sponsored by the British Academy. His current work focuses on religious pluralism and spiritual agency, and he pioneered the undergraduate teaching of Psychology of Religion and Spirituality at De La Salle University. Despite his otherwise boring academic profile, Darren enjoys comedy, often to the point of attempting a joke and eventually becoming the joke himself. He also considers himself a bit of a Bible geek, although this expertise has yet to translate into consistent victories in social media debates with random Jesus fans.
